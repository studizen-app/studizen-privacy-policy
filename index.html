Studizen Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what the Studizen Chrome extension stores, how its features use that information, and when information leaves your device.

Information stored in your browser

Studizen stores extension data in Chrome local extension storage. Some interface preferences, drafts, and imported-music metadata use this browser's local storage; short-lived interface and handoff state may use session storage. Studizen does not use a Studizen account or cloud-sync service. Chrome or your device may separately back up or manage browser-profile data according to your own settings.

Depending on the features you use, local records can include:

  • Your optional name, study profile, selected theme, focus defaults, sound preferences, and other settings.
  • Courses and libraries you create, including course titles, subjects, library assignments, and links; notes, checklists, daily goals, and completed tasks you enter.
  • Focus-session identifiers, course and subject associations, start/end times, active study intervals, planned and actual durations, status, and related progress. Analytics and Daily Goals use local session and daily records.
  • XP, rewards, badges, streaks, and other study-progress records.
  • When site protection is used, limited local protection events such as a site host, timestamp, event type, and reason. These event records do not retain the full page path or query string.
  • If Camera Focus Check is used, attention state and diagnostic metadata described below.
  • Music preferences and metadata for audio files that you choose to import.

Focus sessions and analytics

Focus records are used to show your timers, study history, goals, analytics, streaks, and rewards. Active study intervals are used to exclude paused time and scheduled breaks where the session records provide those intervals. New focus-history entries are normally limited to the most recent 300 records; imported or previously stored history may be retained up to the extension's normalization limit. Daily aggregate records are retained until you use the applicable data controls, clear extension data, or remove the extension.

Reward history is limited by the app to recent records. Individual notes, courses, goals, and tasks remain in local browser storage until you delete them or clear the relevant Studizen data. An exported backup may contain the records included by the export feature and is saved wherever you choose; anyone with access to that file may be able to read it.

Website protection and browsing activity

Before first enabling Distraction Protection, Studizen explains that it checks website hosts to apply your selected blocking rules and that limited site-protection event details may be stored locally in this browser. Protection remains off unless you explicitly accept and enable it. Declining leaves protection off and does not change your saved blocking rules; you can turn protection off at any time.

While protection is enabled with that consent, Studizen reads HTTP and HTTPS tab addresses locally to compare hosts with your saved rules and course links, and may redirect a disallowed page. Background redirects and site-protection event recording require both the enabled setting and accepted consent. The code does not build or send a general browsing-history report to a Studizen server.

For applicable protection events in regular browser windows, Studizen can retain a limited local event record containing the site host, time, event type, and reason. The code limits these event logs to recent records (under 24 hours and up to 120 entries per event bucket). Incognito Protection is optional, requires you to enable Studizen in Chrome's Incognito settings, and does not persist Incognito blocked-site events.

When you open a website or course link, the browser sends the ordinary network request to that website. That communication is between your browser and the destination and is not a Studizen analytics upload.

YouTube course titles

When you choose Studizen's YouTube right-click command, the extension uses the selected video's URL and locally available page or thumbnail title to prefill the existing Add Course form. If no matching title is available locally, the form shows a disclosure and two choices. Only if you click "Look up title on YouTube" does Studizen send that selected video's URL directly to YouTube's oEmbed endpoint to request its title. No oEmbed request is made before that action or when you choose "Enter title manually". This is a separate choice for each video and is not saved as a standing permission. YouTube may receive the requested video URL and ordinary request information; the request omits YouTube account credentials.

If you decline the lookup, you can type the title and continue using the form. The course is not saved automatically: its title and link are added to your local Studizen course data only when you submit the Add Course form. Studizen does not send the saved course to a Studizen server or a third-party analytics service.

Camera Focus Check

Camera Focus Check is optional. Before requesting camera access, Studizen displays an in-product disclosure about on-device frame processing and local storage of derived attention status and diagnostics. If you decline, camera access is not requested. If you accept, Studizen saves your choice and then asks Chrome for camera permission with audio disabled; this brief permission check immediately stops the temporary camera stream. Camera Focus Check is enabled only after consent is saved and camera permission is granted. Live camera frames are then processed on your device by the extension's bundled attention-detection components during an active study phase. Monitoring stops when the feature or session stops, the session is paused, or a scheduled break begins.

Studizen does not record or store camera frames or audio, and the extension does not upload camera frames, video, or audio. It may store the derived attention state, status or error reason, timestamps, reminder/distraction counts, and diagnostic measurements such as confidence/evidence scores, detection or inference latency, and inference rate. These derived records are stored locally for the feature and its status display; they are not sent to Studizen or a remote model service. If enabled, reminders may use Chrome notifications or the device's local text-to-speech facility.

Music and imported files

Music settings and imported-track metadata are stored locally. An audio file you select is played from your device; Studizen does not upload it. The browser may not preserve a temporary file reference after restart, so you may need to select an imported file again.

External services and sharing

The extension code reviewed for this policy contains no Studizen account backend or third-party analytics/advertising endpoint. The extension's identified external data request is the conditional YouTube oEmbed title lookup described above. Opening course links or other websites also causes ordinary browser requests to those destinations. Other information described here is handled in your browser. Studizen does not sell this information or use it for targeted advertising.

This describes the extension's current code and does not change the privacy practices of Chrome, YouTube, websites you visit, or other software on your device. Those services have their own policies.

Your choices and data removal

You can turn optional features on or off in Studizen Settings. Camera access is requested only after your action. Chrome controls Incognito access, which you must grant in Chrome's extension settings. You can delete records using Studizen's available data controls, clear the extension's browser data, or remove the extension. Exported backups are separate files and are not deleted when in-extension data is cleared.

Storage security

Studizen stores its records using browser storage and does not add a separate encryption layer to those records. Protect access to your device and Chrome profile, and keep exported backup files in a location you trust. This policy does not make a guarantee about Chrome, operating-system, backup, or device security.
